## Title: Getting Over It (Elias Kim)

## Setup and Interaction Instructions

Click on the two options given at the starting screen and make different choices and decisions to either get through the scenarios or lose and have to restart.

## What Changed

I added a new enemy screen, a timer, as well as a new ending screen for when the timer runs out.

## Assets

No external assets used. All visuals are generated with p5.js.
